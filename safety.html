🛡️ SAFETY REMINDERS
🛡️
SAFETY FIRST
Your safety is our top priority. Please observe the following safety practices at all times while working in the warehouse.
🦺
WEAR YOUR PPE
Wear the required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) at all times.
🚶
KEEP WALKWAYS CLEAR
Keep walkways, aisles, and emergency exits clear of obstructions.
🔍
INSPECT EQUIPMENT
Perform daily equipment inspections before operating forklifts, pallet jacks, or other warehouse equipment.
📦
STACK MATERIALS SAFELY
Stack and store materials properly to prevent items from falling.
🚨
REPORT HAZARDS
Report any unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, or near-miss incidents immediately to the safety officers or supervisor.
🏋️
LIFT PROPERLY
Practice proper lifting techniques and ask for assistance when handling heavy loads.
🧹
GOOD HOUSEKEEPING
Maintain good housekeeping by keeping your work area clean and organized.
⛔
FOLLOW PROCEDURES
Never bypass safety procedures or operate equipment without proper authorization.
🤝
SAFETY IS EVERYONE'S RESPONSIBILITY
Let's work together to maintain a safe, clean, and accident-free workplace.
🏠
THINK SAFETY
Think Safety.
Work Safely.
Go Home Safely.
